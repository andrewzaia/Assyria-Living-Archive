# Heritage preservation starter materials

These are **original, public, blank planning documents**. They are copied to the generated static website and may be downloaded by visitors. They are not completed agreements, licensed media, privately submitted narratives or legal advice.

- `story-planning-template.md` - structured prompts for a sourced, non-sensitive record
- `interview-prompts.md` - optional open-ended oral-history interview questions
- `permissions-review-checklist.md` - separate consent, access, publication and stewardship checks

## Editorial boundaries

Do not add submitted photos, recordings, names or family histories to this folder. The public heritage index is `data/heritage-records.json`: it contains only external published reference descriptions and source IDs. Every item remains at its own custodian. The source record is not a grant of copyright or publication rights.

Public descriptions may include institutional names and already publicly documented narrators when relevant to the cited source. Do not infer access permissions, closed collection details, current addresses or familial relationships. Keep personal submissions offline until an explicit secure intake and review process has been designed and authorised. The existing contribution form only downloads a Markdown draft to the user's device.

The ethical guidance in this milestone references Oral History Australia and the National Library of Australia. Their own policies and terms govern their collections, not this archive's future submissions. Any future interviewer agreement must be developed for its specific repository and jurisdiction; a planning checklist is not a substitute.
